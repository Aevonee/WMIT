# Counsel review — client document templates

Three client-facing templates are drafted for adoption. They are **not yet
wired into the document renderers**: today the system's statement of account
carries only the payment-terms text from Settings, and its tour-voucher
sheet is a service listing — once counsel approves the wording, these
templates become the content the documents carry. They are text-first drafts
that no lawyer has read. **Schedule the review before September 4** —
documents issued at the expo should carry approved wording.

## The package (send these three files)

1. `docs/templates/master-booking-terms-v2-draft.md` — Master Booking Terms:
   the umbrella terms every booking is made under (payment, cancellation,
   liability, acceptance).
2. `docs/templates/package-tour-voucher-template.md` — Package-Specific Tour
   Voucher: the per-trip confirmed schedule issued to clients.
3. `docs/templates/tour-voucher-confirmation-checklist.md` — internal
   checklist staff use before confirming a voucher (include so counsel sees
   the operational controls behind "CONFIRMED").

## What counsel is being asked to decide

- Are the payment, deposit, forfeiture, and cancellation rules enforceable as
  written and consistent with Philippine practice?
- Liability and limitation wording adequate for a travel agency of this size?
- Any mandatory disclosures missing (DTI/BIR-facing receipt language, privacy
  notice reference, visa/passport responsibility wording)?
- Is the voucher's "confirmed vs pending" structure safe to issue before every
  supplier fact is confirmed, given the checklist gates it?
- Anything that must change **before** documents are issued at the September
  expo (vs. nice-to-have later).
