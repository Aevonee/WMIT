For the owner — react to specifics

What changed & why — 8 decisions in this proposal
  1. One command bar, not two. The event selector, sign-up form link, staff identity and sign-out now live in a single navy header. Today a separate blue event strip sits under the header and duplicates its job; merging them frees roughly 70px of vertical space on phones — one more follow-up row visible at the booth.
  2. The funnel is drawn as a funnel. Ten look-alike metric boxes made staff compute the story themselves. Stage bars with proportional widths plus stage-to-stage conversion rates say it directly: 128 leads → 11 bookings, 8.6% — readable from a metre away on the tablet.
  3. Numbers get a display register. Metric values step up to 20–27px tabular figures while working text stays at 12–13px, as in the current ledger. Money and counts read instantly without inflating the whole page.
  4. Two quiet elevation levels. Panels lift a hairline off the chart-paper background and the sticky header casts a soft shadow once you scroll. This replaces the strictly flat surfaces — sections now separate by depth instead of heavier borders, and it stays subtle enough for an operations tool.
  5. Follow-up urgency you can see from a distance. Overdue items sort first, take a red rule and wash (words + colour, never colour alone), and the overdue count rides the tab itself. WhatsApp and Viber keep their brand colours but become thumb-sized buttons with icons.
  6. Manual lead capture is promoted to the page's hero form. It is always open — no disclosure to unfold — with 40px-plus touch targets and destination suggestions drawn from the package templates. This is the action staff repeat most at the booth; the badge import moves into a collapsed secondary panel.
  7. Accent discipline. Passage Blue becomes the single data accent (funnel, chart, links, progress bars). Manifest Green stays reserved for the one primary action in view and for booked/success states — the same green the BOOKED pill uses today.
  8. Phone-first tables. Leads and quotes reflow into labelled cards below 720px instead of forcing horizontal scroll, and every control keeps a comfortable touch target. The console is used on phones at the booth as much as on the tablet.

Mockup for evaluation only — tab switching and the marked demo interactions (mark done, mark booked, add lead, filters) run locally in the browser; there are no server calls and nothing is saved. Feature set mirrors the live Expo Console (event selector, dashboard, day-1/3/7 follow-ups, leads & import, packages, multi-option quotes); the Events and Analytics tabs would keep their current design in this proposal.
